Privacy Preserving Sentiment Anlaysis based on various Capitals

Developed FedBiLSTM-Net by integrating LSTM with federated learning using the FedAvg algorithm for privacy-preserving sentiment analysis. Trained the model across 20 non-IID clients over 9 rounds, achieving a centralized evaluation accuracy of 80.17%. Co-authored the resulting research paper, published in the International Journal of Social Sciences Bulletin
